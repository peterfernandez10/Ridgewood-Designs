The Artisan Behind the Brand

Casey Poole —
woodworker, engraver, maker of things that last.

Casey Poole started Ridgewood Designs in 2014 out of a small workshop tucked into the Blue Ridge Mountains, twenty minutes outside Asheville. The first piece — a Bear Key Plaque, made as a thank-you for his sister's first house — is still the studio's most-ordered item.

Every piece starts with hand-selected wood. Casey walks the lumberyard himself, looking for boards with the right figure, the right grain direction, the right kind of quiet. The engraving is done in-house — laser for the precision work, hand-painting for the seasonal pieces, hand-routing for the plaques. Nothing leaves the studio without his finish.

The whole operation is small on purpose. Six people, one dog (Maple), one studio. The pieces that ship out are the same pieces that hang in Casey's own house — and the same pieces, ten years on, that customers send photos of still in use.

"I make pieces I'd want to receive. Heavy enough to stay put. Quiet enough to outlast a trend. Personal enough that someone, somewhere, keeps reaching for it."
